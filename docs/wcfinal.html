Each moment is a StatsBomb 360 freeze-frame: the real on-pitch position of every visible player at the instant of an event (a pass, a shot…), on a 120×80 m pitch. There's no continuous ball track and no shirt numbers in this open data, so it's a sequence of true snapshots stepped on the match clock — not smooth tracking. The dot ringed in gold is the player on the ball; the ball sits at the event. Data: StatsBomb Open Data (free, with attribution) — the actual Argentina 3–3 France final, 18 Dec 2022.
