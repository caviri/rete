Every dot is a position row in tracking.rete — a spatiotemporal graph storing the 2D location of each player and the ball, ~5 frames/second, on a 105×68 m pitch (query it in the playground). This page fetches the positions from R2 and interpolates between frames to animate. The finest grain of “over time”, from an open sample match (Metrica Sports, anonymised).
