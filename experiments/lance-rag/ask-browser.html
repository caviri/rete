A knowledge-graph search that runs entirely in your browser — no server, no database.

How it works

  1. A small AI model (in your browser) turns your question into a vector — numbers capturing its meaning.
  2. It does the same for every entity in the graph, then ranks the closest matches.
  3. It walks the graph's links to show how the top answers connect, as a chain of triples.
