Query playground

100% client-side. This single page embeds the WASM engine and the demo datasets (base64) — no server, no app-load network requests. Works offline from file://. It hands a .rete file's bytes to WebAssembly and runs the dictionary, permutation indexes, SPARQL and zstd decode entirely in your browser.
